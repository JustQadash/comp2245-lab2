My life and Computer Science

October 3rd, 2026 by Qadash Charles

Getting into computer science has been an interesting journey for me. When I first started learning about programming, I was curious about how software and websites were created. Learning HTML and CSS has helped me understand how the different parts of a website work together.

One of the things I enjoy most is being able to create something from scratch. Even when I run into problems, figuring out what went wrong and fixing it makes the process rewarding. I am hoping to continue improving my programming skills and eventually use them to create projects of my own.
